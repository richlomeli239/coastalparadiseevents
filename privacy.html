Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

Effective Date: October 2026 • Headquarters: Fort Myers, FL 33919

1. Commitment to Privacy

At Coastal Paradise Events ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information provided through our website (coastalparadiseevents.com). This policy outlines our standards regarding data collection, client communications, mobile phone privacy, and security practices.

2. Information We Collect

When you request an availability check, price estimate, or retainer reservation, we may collect the following voluntarily submitted information:

  • Personal Contact Data: Full name, email address, and telephone/mobile phone number.
  • Event Specifics: Event date, venue or city location, event category (Wedding, Corporate, Resort, Bar, Private Party), guest count, and selected entertainment/lighting production add-ons.
  • Billing Data: Client billing name and digital invoice correspondence records (all credit card processing is conducted off-site via encrypted third-party processors).

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected details are used solely to deliver premium event production services: verifying Southwest Florida calendar availability, drafting tailored investment estimates, generating written event service agreements, coordinating timelines with your vendor team, and communicating regarding your celebration.

4. Strict Mobile Privacy & Non-Disclosure Policy (10DLC / TCPA)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will strictly NOT be shared with, sold to, rented to, or transferred to any third parties under any circumstances. By providing your mobile telephone number on our forms, in emails, or by sending an initial text message to (941) 500-2566, you consent to receive customer service phone calls and text messages (SMS/MMS) from Coastal Paradise Events.

6. Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

Our site may utilize basic analytics and conversion measurement cookies (such as Google Analytics or Google Ads conversion tracking) to understand website traffic, measure form completion effectiveness, and optimize search engine visibility. These tools gather aggregated, anonymized browsing telemetry and do not capture personal financial credentials.

7. Data Protection & Security

We enforce administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect submitted client records from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Web transmissions on coastalparadiseevents.com are secured using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.
